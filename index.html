Stay in control

Responsible play starts with a plan.

Lottery games are games of chance. No number system or prediction can guarantee a win. Keep your wellbeing and finances first.

  • Set a firm spending limit before playing.
  • Never borrow money or chase losses.
  • Check results and prize claims only with official lottery sources.
  • Take a break if play stops feeling fun.

Frequently asked questions

Is Online Pball Trust the official Powerball website?

No. We are an independent information resource and are not affiliated with the official Powerball lottery. Use official sources for current rules and results.

Can you guarantee winning numbers?

No. Lottery outcomes are random, and no person or system can guarantee winning numbers or a prize.

Where should I verify draw results?

Visit the official Powerball website or the authorised lottery operator in your jurisdiction.
