About this website

Information to help you make informed decisions.

Online Pball Trust is being developed as an independent information website. It is not the official Powerball website, a lottery operator, or a government agency.

We do not verify or guarantee individual winnings. If you receive a message saying you have won a prize, confirm it directly with the official lottery organization before responding.

Stay safe online

Three ways to spot a suspicious prize message

Scammers often use urgency and promises of money to pressure people into sharing information or paying fees.

01

Upfront payment requests

Be wary of requests for release fees, taxes, gift cards, or transfers before a prize can be received. Verify procedures with the official operator.

02

Requests for secrets

Never give anyone your password, bank PIN, full card security details, or one-time verification code to claim a prize.

03

Pressure to act quickly

Unexpected messages demanding immediate action are a reason to stop and independently verify the sender and claim.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions to ask first.

Is this the official Powerball website?

No. This site is independent and is not affiliated with Powerball or any official lottery operator.

Can this website confirm that I won?

No. Only the official lottery operator can confirm a valid ticket or prize claim. Use contact details found independently on its official website.

Should I pay a fee to receive an unexpected prize?

Do not pay based on a message or a link sent to you. Check the official lottery's published claim rules independently first.
